## 10. HOW BOOKING WORKS

**Step 1 — Submit your inquiry.** Send as much detail as possible so an accurate quote can be built. Required fields: event type (graduation party, wedding, etc.) · event date · event location, full address · guest count · items of interest · name, phone number and email. The more detail provided, the better the setup can be planned.

**Step 2 — Receive your quote and contract.** Details are reviewed and a custom quote is sent. Once approved, a contract and invoice follow.

**Step 3 — Secure your date.** The date is secured once the contract is signed and payment is made. Peak season books up quickly — locking in early is recommended.

**Step 4 — Final details and event setup.** About 1–2 weeks before the event, final details and timing are confirmed. Tents are typically set up the day before. Pickup is the day after. Tables and chairs are delivered and left neatly stacked unless setup is requested.

> **Design note:** these four steps map directly onto the Aceternity `Timeline` component. The Step 1 field list is the exact schema for the quote form.

## 11. LAKESIDE WEDDING MANAGEMENT

### Positioning

Lakeside Wedding Management is a wedding coordination add-on offered **exclusively to couples booking rentals with Lakeside Party Rental**. It is for couples who want more than rentals — someone keeping the moving pieces organised so the day comes together smoothly.

From coordinating with vendors and keeping the timeline on track to overseeing details on the wedding day, Lakeside manages the logistics behind the scenes. Because they are already involved with the rentals, they understand the setup, the space, and the details of the event, making coordination feel more connected start to finish.

**Tagline:** *Your rentals. Your wedding. One team helping bring it all together.*

### Why couples add wedding management

Headline: **You've planned the wedding. You shouldn't have to manage it.**

Your wedding day shouldn't be spent answering vendor questions, watching the clock, directing setup, checking table placement, or figuring out what happens next. Lakeside Wedding Management gives you someone who already understands your rentals, layout, setup and wedding day plan.

Six supporting points:

1. **One team, fewer moving pieces** — your rental and wedding management teams are already connected.
2. **A timeline someone actually manages** — vendors, important moments and transitions kept moving throughout the day.
3. **Your setup is already familiar** — they know the tents, tables, chairs, décor and layouts involved.
4. **Vendor coordination** — someone communicating with vendors, keeping everyone on the same page.
5. **Less work for your family** — parents, wedding party and friends should be celebrating, not managing.
6. **Support when plans change** — higher collections add hands-on support for weather plans, décor, vendors, complex timelines and the unexpected.
